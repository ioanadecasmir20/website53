About Guarnest

A security company built around clear standards, experienced people, practical site knowledge and accountable service delivery.

Who we are

Professional security starts with dependable people and clear systems.

Guarnest Security provides guarding and site security support for organisations that need a visible, well-managed and professional presence. We take time to understand the site, the operating environment and the responsibilities attached to each assignment before personnel are deployed.

Our officers are selected for professionalism, reliability and communication. Where a role is licensable under the Private Security Industry Act, the individual assigned to that role must hold the appropriate current SIA licence. Experience, presentation, role suitability and assignment requirements are considered as part of deployment planning.

  • Appropriate current SIA licences for licensable roles
  • Experienced security personnel matched to assignment needs
  • Site-specific instructions, escalation routes and reporting standards
  • Professional conduct, presentation and customer awareness

Operational oversight

Supervision that continues after deployment.

A strong security service needs active management, not just a rota. Our supervisors and operational staff support assignments through communication, checks, reporting and performance oversight.

01

Supervisor oversight

Supervisors support mobilisation, monitor attendance and standards, review incidents and help resolve operational issues as they arise.

02

Smart monitoring

Where appropriate, our monitoring and reporting tools can support patrol verification, check-ins, incident records, handovers and evidence of completed site activity.

03

Performance checks

We use client feedback, supervisor contact, reporting quality and site observations to identify where standards need reinforcing or the brief needs adjusting.

Our approach

Good security is consistent when nobody is watching.

Our work is based on preparation, visibility, communication and accountability. Officers should understand why they are on site, what needs attention, what good performance looks like and when an issue must be escalated.

Assignment instructions are designed around the client environment rather than copied from a generic template. When circumstances change, we review the service so that deployment remains proportionate and useful.

What clients can expect

A structured service from mobilisation onward.

01

Understand

We review the premises, operating hours, access arrangements, known risks and service objectives.

02

Plan

We agree duties, reporting expectations, escalation routes, presentation requirements and deployment arrangements.

03

Deploy

Suitable personnel are briefed against the assignment and supported through operational supervision.

04

Monitor

Performance, incidents and client feedback are reviewed so the service can be maintained and improved.
