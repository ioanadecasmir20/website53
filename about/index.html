About Guarnest

A security company built around clear standards, practical site knowledge and professional conduct.

Who we are

A dependable security partner starts with the basics.

Guarnest Security is based in Ipswich, Suffolk and provides security services for organisations that need a visible, well-managed and professional presence.

We take a straightforward approach. Before deployment, the service should have a clear purpose, clear instructions and a clear line of communication. That helps personnel understand what good performance looks like and helps clients know what to expect.

  • Professional conduct and presentation
  • Site-specific instructions rather than one-size-fits-all briefs
  • Appropriate checks for personnel and assignments
  • Proportionate incident escalation and reporting

Our approach

Good security is consistent when nobody is watching.

Our work is based on preparation, visibility, communication and accountability. We aim to provide security personnel who understand the environment they are working in and the standard expected of them.

Where an assignment requires an SIA licence, personnel must hold the appropriate current licence for the role.
