Careers at Guarnest

Professional security depends on professional people. We are interested in applicants who take standards, communication, reliability and service seriously.

Work with us

Build a career where professionalism matters.

Security work is about more than standing at a post. It requires awareness, judgement, communication, consistency and the ability to remain professional when circumstances change.

Our contracts can include guarding, retail, corporate, construction, event, concierge, mobile and site-based assignments. Vacancies vary by location, shift pattern and client requirement.

For licence-linked roles, applicants must hold the correct current SIA licence for the position and demonstrate their right to work in the UK. We also value reliable attendance, accurate reporting, smart presentation and the ability to work as part of a wider operational team.

Before you apply

Help us understand your experience.

A complete enquiry helps us assess whether your experience and availability match current or upcoming assignments.

01

Current CV

Include recent employment history, relevant security assignments, responsibilities and accurate contact details.

02

Licence & training

For SIA-licensed positions, include your licence type, expiry date and any relevant role-specific training.

03
