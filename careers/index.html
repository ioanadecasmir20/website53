Careers at Guarnest

Professional security depends on professional people. We are interested in applicants who take standards, communication and reliability seriously.

Work with us

Bring the right attitude to the assignment.

Security work is about more than standing at a post. It requires awareness, judgement, communication and the ability to remain professional when circumstances change.

Vacancies vary by contract and location. For licence-linked roles, applicants must hold the correct current SIA licence for the position and be able to demonstrate their right to work in the UK.

  • Professional presentation and communication
  • Reliability and punctuality
  • Ability to follow site procedures and report clearly
  • Appropriate current SIA licence where required
