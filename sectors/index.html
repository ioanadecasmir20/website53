Site-specific security

One brief should not be copied from one site to the next.

A warehouse may prioritise perimeter, vehicle and gate activity. A retail site may place greater weight on loss prevention and customer interaction. A corporate environment may require a discreet front-of-house presence.

We use the client brief and site information to define duties, reporting expectations and escalation arrangements around the actual environment.
