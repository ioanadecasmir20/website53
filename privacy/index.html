Privacy notice

A plain-language website privacy notice for Guarnest Security.

Last updated: September 2026

Information you provide

If you contact us, we may receive your name, contact details and the information included in your enquiry. If you apply for work, you may also provide employment, licence and recruitment information.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, discuss requested services, manage business relationships, consider recruitment enquiries and meet legal or regulatory obligations where applicable.

Website data

This version of the website does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. If analytics, cookies or third-party form services are added later, this notice should be updated before they are enabled.

Sharing and retention

Information should only be shared where needed for a legitimate business, contractual or legal purpose. It should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose and any applicable record-keeping requirements.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights over personal information held about you, including access, correction and deletion rights. Contact us if you have a privacy question or request.
